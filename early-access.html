Questions

Do I need a Mac?

Yes. In early access Anthrie runs on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) on macOS 26. The Mac is where it can read your mail, messages, calls, notes and files in place — things no phone app is allowed to see. iPhone, Apple Watch and iPad follow.

What will it read?

Only what you choose: Mail, Messages, calls from your iPhone, Calendar, Contacts, Reminders, Notes, WhatsApp's notifications, and the folders you pick. It shows you each one, why, and what it will never do with it, before asking for anything.

I use Gmail or Outlook in a browser. Will it work?

Yes. Connect the account to your Mac once (System Settings › Internet Accounts) with Mail, Calendars and Contacts on, and keep reading it wherever you like. Your Mac keeps a copy that Anthrie can read; Anthrie keeps Mail syncing quietly in the background.

Does it send anything on my behalf?

Not in early access: it only reads. It never sends, pays, signs, books or deletes. Acting on your behalf comes later, one kind of task at a time, and only with your tap.

Does my data leave my Mac?

No. In early access everything Anthrie reads and learns stays on your Mac, encrypted. It uses Apple's on-device model; no cloud model or server of ours sees your data.

How long does it take to get going?

About ten minutes to set up. The first time, Anthrie reads your history — usually 20 to 40 minutes on a Mac with years of mail — and tells you as it goes. Your first brief appears when it's done.

What does it cost?

Early access is free. We'll tell you about pricing well before it changes, and you can always erase everything and leave.

What can't it see?

Apps outside your Mac's own — like Notion, or WhatsApp on your phone alone. It tells you plainly what it can and can't see.

How do I leave?

Erase everything from inside the app, then drag it to the Bin. Your own mail, messages and files are never touched.
