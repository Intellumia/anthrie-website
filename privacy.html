Privacy policy

Anthrie exists to take the weight of your digital life off you. To do that it has to read a lot about you. This policy says exactly what it reads, why, where that data goes, and what it will never do with it.

Effective 24 September 2026. This version: 28 September 2026. Anthrie is in private early access; this policy describes what Anthrie does today. We will update it before anything new is introduced.

Who we are

Anthrie is made by Intellumia Pte. Ltd., a company registered in Singapore (UEN 202508003K), at 60 Paya Lebar Road, #07-54 Paya Lebar Square, Singapore 409051 ("Intellumia", "we", "us"). We are the controller (under India's law, the data fiduciary) of the personal data described here.

Our Data Protection Officer, who is also our Grievance Officer, is Sundeep Kumar. Write to him at privacy@anthrie.com about anything in this policy, to use your rights, or to complain. We acknowledge every message within 7 days and answer in full within 30 days.

Our promises

  • Your data works only for you. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never use it to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.
  • No person reads your data, including our staff. In early access it never leaves your Mac, so we have no way to see it. If you ask us for help, we see only what you choose to show us.
  • Anthrie never deletes anything of yours and never moves or changes your files. In early access it only reads: it never sends, pays, signs, calls or deletes anything.
  • You can see where your data went. In early access it stays on your Mac. If that ever changes, we will tell you first, and Anthrie will show you every service and AI model that processed it, when, and why.

What Anthrie reads, and only with your permission

Anthrie reads only the sources you allow on your Mac, and you can switch any of them off at any time.

  • Email held by your Mac's Mail app (for example iCloud Mail, Gmail, Outlook added to your Mac): messages and their attachments, their senders and recipients, and their state (read, flagged, where they are filed). Text in attachments such as PDFs and scanned documents is read on your Mac. Bank and card statements are not opened.
  • Messages (iMessage and SMS) and, on Mac, WhatsApp's notifications, read-only. Anthrie never sends on WhatsApp or anywhere else.
  • Calls: who, when, how long and which direction, from your Mac's call history.
  • Calendars, contacts and reminders.
  • Notes and files: the titles of your notes, and the names, dates and sizes of files in your home folder or the folders you choose instead. Anthrie never moves, renames, changes or deletes a file, and never opens a file or folder that holds passwords, keys or certificates.
  • Identity documents: when you ask for a document's number, and only after Touch ID, Anthrie reads it from the document's scan on your Mac and shows it to you once. The number is not kept.
  • What you tell Anthrie: what you say or type to it, and what you correct, so it can learn how you work. Your voice is recognised on your Mac and the audio is not kept.

Anthrie does not read Slack, Telegram, photos, meetings, bank or card accounts in early access. If we add any source, we will describe it here first and it will be off until you switch it on.

Why it reads them

To help you: find what needs your attention, answer your questions from what you already have, keep track of what is open with the people who matter to you, and learn who and what matters to you so it brings you only what it should. In early access Anthrie only reads: it never sends, pays, signs or deletes anything. We process this data to provide the service you asked for (performance of our contract with you) and, where required, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act, that is your consent, which you give by switching each source on and withdraw by switching it off.

No decision about you is made by automated means alone. A person at Intellumia reads every early-access request and decides whom to invite.

Where your data goes

In early access, your data is processed on your own Mac and nowhere else. What Anthrie learns is kept on your Mac, encrypted, with a key held in your Mac's Keychain. There is no Anthrie cloud service, and none of what Anthrie reads or learns is synced to your other devices or to iCloud. (If iCloud Keychain is on, a locked copy of Anthrie's key may be kept there; only this Mac can unlock it.)

To understand what it reads and to answer you, Anthrie uses Apple's models on your Mac. Your voice is recognised and spoken on your Mac, and the audio is not kept.

No cloud AI model is used in early access. Thinking with cloud models is paused on every Mac, including our founder's own. When it resumes, it will apply to our founder's own Mac only, as a test chosen by him: there, anything except secrets may be sent, one item or one situation at a time, through OpenRouter (United States) to model providers that are required to keep no copy and never to train on it, and every call is logged on that Mac. OpenRouter keeps records of each request, such as its time, the model used and its size. An early-access Mac cannot be set up this way. We will not use any cloud model with your data unless we have told you first, listed the provider here, and you have switched it on.

The services that process personal data for us today:

  • Google Cloud (Google), in Singapore: early-access requests, voice notes and the short-lived request logs described below.
  • GitHub, Inc., in the United States: hosts this website.
  • Google Workspace (Google): our email. It holds the messages you send to hello@anthrie.com and privacy@anthrie.com, and our replies.

We will list any change here before it takes effect.

Where it is processed, and transfers between countries

Early-access requests are stored in Singapore. The website is served by GitHub from the United States and from servers near you. So if you live elsewhere, for example in India, the European Union or the United Kingdom, some of your data is processed outside your country. Where the law requires it, we rely on the providers' data protection terms, including the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum to them, which bind them to protect your data to a standard comparable to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards that apply.

This website

anthrie.com sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and runs no analytics, advertising, session recording or tracking of any kind, so there is nothing to accept and no cookie banner. Its font, pictures and films come from anthrie.com itself, never from another company's servers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. To serve the pages, GitHub receives your IP address and your browser's details when you visit, and handles them under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or keep them.

Your microphone is used only if you choose to record a voice note on the early-access form, only while you are recording, and nothing is sent until you have played it back and ticked the box to agree. Nothing you type is recorded as you type it; the form sends only what you submit.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We do not track you across websites and do not sell or share your personal information, so a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal changes nothing here: the site behaves the same with or without one. If we ever did anything a Global Privacy Control signal asks us not to do, we would honour the signal as your request to opt out.

Google user data

In early access Anthrie reads Google mail, calendars and contacts only through your Mac's own apps, and does not connect to Google itself. If we offer a direct Google connection later and you connect a Google account, Anthrie will read the Gmail, Calendar, Contacts and Drive data you authorise, only to provide the features described above to you. Anthrie's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train generalised AI models, and do not allow humans to read it except with your explicit consent for a specific message, for security purposes, to comply with law, or where the data is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

Early access requests

When you ask for early access on anthrie.com we keep what you give us in the form, together with when you asked:

  • your name and email address, which Mac you have and where you read your mail. We need these to handle your request; everything else below is optional;
  • that you confirmed you are 18 or older, and when;
  • a record of your consent: when you sent the form, which version of this policy and of our terms it linked to, and whether you sent a voice note with its own consent. It is how we can show what you agreed to;
  • if you choose to answer, the options you tap: what you want from an AI, what bothers you, what took your time that day, what you wish it did, what you pay for today to automate or hand things off (the kinds of apps and help, by name), and roughly how much a month;
  • if you choose to, a few words in your own writing;
  • if you choose to, a voice note of up to a minute, recorded in your browser. It is sent only if you play it back and tick the box that agrees to it, and we keep how long it is and when you agreed.

We use all of it only to decide whom to invite, to write to you about early access, and to understand what people need from Anthrie. We will email you only about early access (your invitation, and news about early access itself), and about nothing else unless you say yes. We rely on your consent, given when you send the form, and you can withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it: one email to privacy@anthrie.com, or a reply to any email from us. Your answers and any words are stored in Intellumia's own Google Cloud project in Singapore (Firestore). A voice note is stored as a private file in the same project, in Singapore (Cloud Storage); only the Anthrie team can open it, to listen to your request, and it is never used to train any AI or to recognise your voice. Our record does not include your IP address, and we set no cookies and do not track you; Google Cloud's own request logs note the IP address and browser each request came from, and delete them after 30 days.

Ask us at privacy@anthrie.com and we will delete your request, any voice note and the record of your consent, within 30 days. Otherwise we delete them within 12 months of the end of early access, and a voice note is deleted automatically 18 months after you sent it in any case.

How long we keep it

  • What Anthrie has learned is kept on your Mac, encrypted, until you erase it. We hold no copy.
  • Your voice: the audio is not kept; what you said is kept, like anything else you tell Anthrie.
  • Erase everything Anthrie keeps from inside the app, in one step, at any time. Your own email, files and other source data are yours and are never touched.
  • Early-access requests and the record of your consent: until you ask us to delete them, and at the latest 12 months after early access ends.
  • Voice notes: until you ask, 12 months after early access ends, or 18 months after you sent one, whichever comes first.
  • Google Cloud's request logs for the form: 30 days. Emails you send us: as long as we need them to answer you and keep a record of what you asked, and no longer than 24 months.

Your rights

You can see what Anthrie knows about you and correct it; see what each source was used for; switch off any source; and erase your Anthrie data, all from inside the app. Because it is kept only on your Mac, we cannot see, export or erase it for you; ask us and we will help you do it yourself.

For what we do hold (your early-access request, any voice note and emails you send us), you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it, or bring it up to date;
  • delete it, and stop using it;
  • withdraw your consent, which is as easy as giving it: one email is enough, and it does not affect what we did before you withdrew it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it, or have it sent to you or someone else in a common format;
  • let someone else act for you (for example, under India's law, a person you nominate in case of your death or incapacity).

Write to privacy@anthrie.com from the address you used, or tell us which one it was, so we can check the request is yours. We acknowledge within 7 days and answer in full within 30 days. We will not treat you any differently for using these rights. It costs nothing.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the regulator where you live: in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Commission; in India, the Data Protection Board of India, after first raising it with our Grievance Officer; in the European Union, your local data protection authority; in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office.

Security

What Anthrie keeps is encrypted with a key held in your Mac's Keychain that only this Mac can unlock, except the list of words it searches by, which relies on your Mac's FileVault disk encryption, so please keep FileVault on. Anthrie has read-only access to your sources, never opens secrets or bank and card statements, and no server of ours holds your data.

Early-access requests travel over an encrypted connection and are kept in a private Google Cloud project, encrypted at rest, that only the Anthrie team can open. Voice notes are kept in a private store with no public access. If a breach ever put your personal data at risk, we would tell you and the regulators the law requires, as quickly as the law requires. To report a security problem, see our security contact.

Features that are not available yet

Our website describes some features as coming soon, such as apps for iPhone, Apple Watch and iPad, follow-ups sent from Anthrie's own address, acting on your behalf, and direct connections to Google or Microsoft accounts. They are not part of Anthrie today, and this policy does not yet cover them. Before any of them is introduced we will update this policy to describe what it reads, where the data goes and which processors are used, and tell you in the app. Each will be off until you switch it on, and each will keep these rules: any model or service that processes your data keeps no copy of it, beyond a short-lived cache in memory, and never trains on it; information about health or money does not leave your Mac; anything stored away from your Mac is encrypted with a key that only your Anthrie holds; and every service and model that processes your data is recorded where you can see it.

Children

Anthrie is only for people aged 18 or over. The early-access form asks you to confirm that you are, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has sent us their details, write to privacy@anthrie.com with the email address they used. We will delete it and confirm when it is done, without asking you for anything more than we need to find it.

Changes

When we change this policy, we post the new version here with its date. If you use Anthrie, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect; if you have asked for early access, we will email you before a change that matters takes effect. We will never apply a change that weakens these promises to data we already hold without your consent. You can ask us for any earlier version.
