WHERE THIS GOES

Every company will have a memory of its own.

Anthrie starts with founder-led companies. The thesis is bigger: the institutional memory and judgment layer of the organization — every decision, its reasoning, and its outcome, compounding into how the company decides. In a 250-2,000-person organization, that is what makes a Chief of Staff superhuman, and what a transformation program installs: a consultant's diagnosis is stale the day they leave; the decision record is still accurate in month six. Models get smarter for everyone. This gets smarter for you.

If you run a strategy office, a Chief of Staff function, or a PE portfolio and want to shape this instrument, talk to us.
