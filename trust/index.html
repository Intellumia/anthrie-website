TRUST

Built to be trusted by the whole team.

01

Disclosed, always

Anthrie runs as team memory the team knows about. People in ingested channels are told before anything is shared. We decline pilots structured as covert monitoring.

02

Evidence-linked, always

Every claim traces to a source message or is explicitly marked as inference. "Show me where this came from" always has an answer.

03

Corrections with names on them

When someone marks a claim wrong, that correction is an attributed, timestamped event in an append-only record — never a silent edit.

04

Bounded and deletable

You choose the sample. Raw exports are deleted on an agreed date. Because every claim links to a person and a source, removing someone's data removes everything derived from it.

No OAuth, no connectors, no live access to your systems, no multi-tenant infrastructure. Your sanitized sample is processed on the engagement machine; only the extraction step sends that text to Anthropic's API, which does not train on it. Every run is stamped with the model and prompt version that produced it.
