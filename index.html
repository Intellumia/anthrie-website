01 · THE PROBLEM

Everyone selling you AI earns more when you spend more.

Software vendors, agencies, consultancies: every incentive in the room points at a bigger bill. So pilots stall, tools go unused, and the Monday report still takes three days. MIT's 2025 study of enterprise GenAI found 95% of pilots produced no measurable P&L impact.

The missing piece is not technology. It is judgment on your side of the table: which workflows will pay, who should build them, and who answers when the number does not move.

That judgment is what we sell. Nothing else. No licences. No build shop. No headcount to keep billable.

05 · WHERE AI PAYS

Patterns we see in founder-led companies.

Not case studies. Patterns: the places AI reliably moves a number in businesses like yours.

MANUFACTURING

Quotes going silent

"You quote 150 to 200 times a month. Nobody can tell you which quotes went quiet, or why you lost."

AI chases every open quotation and surfaces the deals worth reviving.

The number that moves: quote-to-order conversion.

MANUFACTURING

Money stuck outside

"Your cash sits with customers, and follow-up happens only when you escalate."

A follow-up engine that never forgets: every invoice aged, chased and escalated with history attached.

The number that moves: DSO.

DISTRIBUTION

The order desk

"Orders arrive on WhatsApp, phone and email. Your team re-types them all day."

Orders read from any channel, validated against price, credit and stock, and punched into the ERP.

The number that moves: order errors and same-day fulfilment.

PHARMA

Claims that pile up

"Claims, expiry returns and scheme settlements queue for months, and every stockist is unhappy."

Claims read, validated against scheme terms and queued for approval; only exceptions need a human.

The number that moves: settlement cycle time.

REAL ESTATE

Leads that die quietly

"Portals and brokers send hundreds of enquiries. Fresh ones get called; the rest go cold."

Every lead qualified, followed up on cadence and revived when silent.

The number that moves: site visits per hundred enquiries.

ANY INDUSTRY

The stalled pilot

"You paid for AI pilots last year. They are dead, and nobody will say why."

An autopsy against data, adoption and vendor delivery; the survivor goes to production with an owner and a number.

The number that moves: pilots reaching production.
