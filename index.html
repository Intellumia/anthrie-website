01 · THE PROBLEM

Everyone selling you AI earns more when you spend more.

Software vendors, agencies, consultancies: every incentive in the room points at a bigger bill. So pilots stall, tools go unused, and the Monday report still takes three days. MIT's 2025 study of enterprise GenAI found 95% of pilots produced no measurable P&L impact.

The missing piece is not technology. It is judgment on your side of the table: which workflows will pay, who should build them, and who answers when the number does not move.

That judgment is what we sell. Nothing else. No licences. No build shop. No headcount to keep billable.

03 · FIT

Built for owner-led companies.

FIT

You.

The owner runs the company and signs every big decision. Large enough to fund real change. Working systems in place: ERP, CRM or equivalent.

NOT FOR

Not you.

Companies run entirely on Tally and WhatsApp. Not a judgment on the business, just too early for this: AI needs working systems to operate on, and we do not build those systems ourselves. Anyone shopping for a chatbot vendor. Buyers who want a deck, not a decision.

04 · HOW WE WORK

The primary unit of value is a decision.

AI programmes fail when activity outruns judgment. We make the evidence, choice, owner and metric explicit, then preserve what happened so each decision improves the next one.

01

Find.

Interview the people doing the work, inspect the systems and surface the workflows where AI could move a business metric.

02

Decide.

Score value, feasibility, adoption risk and time-to-value. Name the owner, baseline the KPI and choose what earns investment.

03

Deliver.

Put the chosen workflow into production through a vetted specialist partner, with a 90-day plan and active governance.

04

Compound.

Review decisions, assumptions, commitments and outcomes against the baseline every month. Advice evaporates; the record compounds.

Evidence → decision → delivery → measured outcome → better judgment

Patterns we see in owner-led companies.

Not case studies. Patterns: the places AI reliably moves a number in businesses like yours.

MANUFACTURING

Quotes going silent

"You quote 150 to 200 times a month. Nobody can tell you which quotes went quiet, or why you lost."

DISTRIBUTION

The order desk

"Orders arrive on WhatsApp, phone and email. Your team re-types them all day."

PHARMA

Claims that pile up

"Claims, expiry returns and scheme settlements queue for months, and every stockist is unhappy."
