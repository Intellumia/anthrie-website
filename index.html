"Why did we do it this way?"

Someone asks about a call made a year ago. The person who made it has moved on; the reasoning lives in no document. The answer is a shrug — so the decision gets made again, at full price.

02

A company that cannot remember its decisions pays for each one twice — once to make it, and again every time it has to be remade, re-argued, or reconstructed from memory.

FROM THE FOUNDER

Every company runs on decisions. The reasoning behind them—the trade-offs weighed, the risks named, the context that made a call the right one—usually lives in chat threads and a few people’s heads. Knowledge tells you what happened. Judgment tells you what to do. Most tools keep the first and lose the second.

Anthrie exists to keep the judgment — as a record the whole team shares, not a private report. It assembles the evidence behind each decision, links every claim to where it came from, marks what is still inference, and remembers the outcome. Settled decisions stay settled. New people inherit the reasoning, not just the rules. Models get smarter for everyone. Anthrie gets smarter for you — your decisions, your context, your organization.

We are taking a small number of design partners this year, one decision at a time. If this is the problem you feel every week, I would like to talk.
