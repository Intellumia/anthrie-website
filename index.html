01 · THE PROBLEM

Everyone selling you AI earns more when you spend more.

Software vendors, agencies, consultancies: every incentive in the room points at a bigger bill. So pilots stall, tools go unused, and the Monday report still takes three days. MIT's 2025 study of enterprise GenAI found 95% of pilots produced no measurable P&L impact.

The missing piece is not technology. It is judgment on your side of the table: which workflows will pay, who should build them, and who answers when the number does not move.

That judgment is what we sell. Nothing else. No licences. No build shop. No headcount to keep billable.

02 · THE ENGAGEMENT

Start with the AI Value Diagnostic.

Three weeks inside your business. Four deliverables. One honest answer.

If the plan makes sense, we stay on as your operating partner: running the programme, governing the build partners, reporting against the baseline every month. Where the metric is clean, part of our fee rides on it.

Start with a diagnostic
  1. 01

    An opportunity map.

    Ten to fifteen opportunities in your operations, scored on value, feasibility and adoption risk. The top three, detailed.

  2. 02

    A baseline.

    One priority workflow measured properly, so improvement is proven, not claimed.

  3. 03

    A 90-day plan.

    What to build first, through which partner, at what budget.

  4. 04

    A straight recommendation.

    Including "do not spend on AI yet" when that is the truth.
