A twin that runs your digital life alongside you.

So you can live the rest of it.

Anthrie is in private early access.

Your life is spread across inboxes, messengers, calendars, devices and dozens of services, and you are the one holding it all together. Anthrie works in parallel with you, around the clock: it reads what comes in, keeps track of what you promised and what you are owed, prepares replies in your voice, and has everything ready before you ask. You see outcomes, not apps.

It does everything up to the final act

Sending, calling, paying and signing stay with you: one tap, and the tap is informed — who it goes to, what it says, why, and what happens next. Over time you can hand Anthrie more, one kind of task at a time, and take it back with a word.

It earns your trust the way people do

  • CompetenceIt gets things right, and every model it uses has to pass our tests first.
  • DiscretionYour data is encrypted, never exposed, and never seen by a person — including us.
  • LoyaltyIt works only for you. Your data is never sold, never used for ads, never used to train models.
  • AccountabilityEvery action it takes can be seen and undone, and you can see everywhere your data went.
  • JudgementIt knows when to ask. Anything that commits you waits for your tap.

Where it works

Anthrie is built for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac, and connects to the services you already use — your email, calendars, messages and more — with your permission, and only for the parts you choose.
