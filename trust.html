Your data, in plain words

Anthrie is only useful if you can trust it with everything. So here is exactly what it does.

This page is the short version. The legal one is our privacy policy.

What it reads

Only what you choose, from what your Mac's own apps already hold: Mail, Messages, calls from your iPhone, Calendar, Contacts, Reminders, Notes, WhatsApp's notifications, and the folders you pick. Before it asks macOS for any permission, it shows you each source, why it would read it, and what it will never do with it — and you switch off whatever you'd rather keep out.

It reads things where they are. Nothing is moved, copied into your folders, or deleted.

Some things it never opens at all: bank and card statements, passwords, keys and certificates, and anything in a folder named for secrets.

Where it keeps what it learns

On your Mac, encrypted with a key that only your Mac holds. In early access, nothing Anthrie reads or learns leaves your Mac: it uses Apple's on-device model to understand what arrives, and no cloud model or server of ours ever sees your data.

When Anthrie later offers a private cloud — so it can keep working while a MacBook sleeps — it will be your own, sealed so that only your Anthrie can open it, and it will be your choice.

Who can see it

You. No person at Intellumia reads your data. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train anyone's AI.

What it does, and what it never does

  • In early access, it only reads. It never sends, pays, signs, books or deletes anything.
  • Later, anything that commits you — a reply, a payment, a signature — waits for your one informed tap: who it goes to, what it says, why, and what happens next.
  • It never moves money on its own, never deletes anything of yours, and never sends on your personal WhatsApp.

How you stay in control

  • Switch any source off at any time; Anthrie stops opening it at once.
  • See, for each source, how much Anthrie has read and what it was used for.
  • Tell it when it's wrong, with one click.
  • Erase everything Anthrie keeps on your Mac — its memory, its search, its keys — in one click. Your own mail, messages and files are never touched.
